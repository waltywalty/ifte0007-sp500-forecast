# Third-party notices and rights

This private assessment repository has no blanket open-source/Creative Commons licence. Establish authority over the group's own contributions before granting outbound rights; third-party terms remain separate.

The initial code, explanations and invented fixtures are created for this project with disclosed AI assistance. That does not certify originality, ownership or universal clearance. Record subsequently identified adapted fragments in the [rights register](evidence/manifests/rights_register.json) and retain upstream notices here.

No package source, publisher papers, course documents, logos, screenshots, fonts or downloaded templates are intentionally vendored. [References](docs/references.bib) point to sources rather than reproducing them.

Dependencies are installed under their own licences. Before redistributing source/binaries/assets, inspect their applicable obligations and preserve notices; the package lock is not itself permission.

Empirical source histories and derived artifacts require destination-specific review. Private WRDS material, protected holdouts and complete provider histories are not included by default. See [data permissions](docs/data_and_permissions.md).

If an issue is found, stop the affected export and record an authorised correction/withdrawal. Do not claim retroactive permission.
